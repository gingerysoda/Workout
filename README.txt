WORKOUT - install on your iPhone (about 10 minutes, once)

1. On your computer, sign in to github.com (free account).
2. Click + (top right) > New repository. Name it "workout", set it to Public, click Create repository.
3. On the new repo page click "uploading an existing file". Drag in EVERYTHING inside this
   folder (index.html, main.js, styles.css, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest and the icons folder).
   Click Commit changes.
4. In the repo go to Settings > Pages. Under "Branch" pick main and / (root), click Save.
   After a minute the page shows your address: https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/workout/
5. On your iPhone open that address in Safari, tap Share > Add to Home Screen > Add.
6. Open Workout from the home screen. It works offline after the first open.

Updating later: upload the new files to the same repo (replace when asked). The app picks up
the new version the next time you open it twice. Your data stays on the phone.

Back up from Today > gear icon > Save backup (to Files or iCloud Drive) once a week.
